# Privacy Policy for Aelnir Video Compressor

**Effective date: 17 November 2026**

Aelnir ("we", "us") provides the Aelnir Video Compressor mobile application (the
"App"). This policy explains what the App accesses, uses, stores, and shares.

## Summary

The App needs no account and has no server. Every video is compressed, trimmed, and converted on your device, and we never receive your videos or your history. The free App shows ads through Google AdMob, whose SDK collects limited device and usage data to provide, measure, and protect advertising.

## Information processed on your device

Depending on what you use, the App processes:

- Videos you pick in the App or share to it from another app, including the information inside them (such as date, duration, and location, if the video has any)
- The settings you choose for each job (preset, size, resolution, format)
- The compressed or converted files the App creates

This stays on your device. The App works on temporary copies in its private storage, which are removed when a job finishes. It does not upload your videos, and we have no server to upload them to.

## Files the App saves

Results are saved to your device's Movies folder, in an Aelnir album, or to a
place you choose. Your original videos are not changed. Once saved, those files
belong to you and are managed by Android and your other apps, not by us.

## History

The App keeps a local history of jobs so you can find your results. Each entry
records the settings used, the size before and after, the date, the status, and
where the result was saved. It does not store copies of your videos. History
stays on your device. You can delete it in the App, and uninstalling the App
removes it.

## Device permissions

The App asks for as little as it can:

- **Videos and files:** none. You pick videos with Android's own picker or choose a save location yourself, so the App only sees what you choose.
- **Notifications:** show progress and completion of a long job. You can decline this.
- **Foreground service:** keep a long job running while you use another app.
- **Internet and advertising ID:** load ads and obtain advertising consent. Nothing from your videos or history is sent with them.

You can deny the optional permissions and the rest of the App keeps working.

## Advertising

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform.
Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads
are shown, and you can change your choice later in the App's advertising privacy
choices. If you decline, Google may still show limited or non-personalized ads.

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares:

- IP address (which may be used to estimate general location)
- Product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views
- Diagnostic information about App and SDK performance
- Device identifiers, including the advertising ID where available

Google uses this for advertising (including personalized advertising where you
consent), analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data is
encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

We do not sell personal information and run no analytics, account, or cloud
service of our own. Your videos are never given to the ad service.

## Sharing you choose

When you share a result to another app, the file goes to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies.

## Retention and deletion

We keep no videos, history, or personal data on any server. Local history stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. Files saved to your device stay until you delete them. Google keeps advertising data according to its policies and controls.

## Children

The App is a general-audience app and is not designed for children under 13. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Your data is processed and stored on your device, and network traffic from the
ad SDK is encrypted in transit. No method is perfectly secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the App, the law, or our providers change. The
updated policy will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
