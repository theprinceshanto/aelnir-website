# Privacy Policy for Aelnir Metadata Cleaner

**Effective date: 17 November 2026**

Aelnir ("we", "us") provides the Aelnir Metadata Cleaner mobile application (the
"App"). This policy explains what the App accesses, uses, stores, and shares.

## Summary

The App needs no account and has no server. Photos are inspected and cleaned on your device, and we never receive your photos or their metadata. Your originals are opened read-only, and every result is a new file. The free App shows ads through Google AdMob, whose SDK collects limited device and usage data to provide, measure, and protect advertising.

## Information processed on your device

Depending on what you use, the App processes:

- Photos you pick in the App or share to it, and the metadata inside them (such as location, date and time, camera and device, software, author, and descriptions)
- The cleaning mode and options you choose
- The cleaned copies the App creates

This stays on your device. Inspecting and cleaning never use the network. Thumbnails are held in memory only. The App does not upload your photos, and we have no server to upload them to.

## Files the App saves

Cleaned copies are saved to your device's pictures folder or to a place you
choose. Your original photos are never changed. Once saved, those files belong
to you and are managed by Android and your other apps, not by us. Removing
metadata does not remove what is visible in the picture itself, such as faces or
street signs.

## History

The App keeps a local history of cleaning jobs. Each entry records counts,
the cleaning mode, category names such as "Location", timestamps, and where the
copies were saved. It never stores metadata values, such as coordinates, and
never stores copies of your photos. Privacy mode in Settings turns history off.
History stays on your device. You can delete it in the App, and uninstalling the
App removes it.

## Device permissions

The App asks for as little as it can:

- **Photos:** none by default. You pick photos with Android's own photo picker, so the App only sees what you choose.
- **Media location:** requested only after an explanation in the App, and only so it can read and show the location stored in photos you choose. You can decline.
- **Foreground service:** keep a long cleaning job running while you use another app.
- **Internet and advertising ID:** load ads and obtain advertising consent. They are not used by the cleaning engine, and nothing from your photos or metadata is sent with them.

You can deny the optional permissions and the rest of the App keeps working.

## Advertising

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform.
Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads
are shown, and you can change your choice later in the App's advertising privacy
choices. If you decline, Google may still show limited or non-personalized ads.

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares:

- IP address (which may be used to estimate general location)
- Product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views
- Diagnostic information about App and SDK performance
- Device identifiers, including the advertising ID where available

Google uses this for advertising (including personalized advertising where you
consent), analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data is
encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

We do not sell personal information and run no analytics, account, or cloud
service of our own. Ads appear only on the home, history, and result screens, and they never touch your photos or their metadata.

## Sharing you choose

When you share a cleaned photo to another app, the file goes to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies. "Share summary" shares only counts and category names.

## Retention and deletion

We keep no photos, metadata, history, or personal data on any server. Local history stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. Files saved to your device stay until you delete them. Google keeps advertising data according to its policies and controls.

## Children

The App is a general-audience app and is not designed for children under 13. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Your data is processed and stored on your device, and network traffic from the
ad SDK is encrypted in transit. No method is perfectly secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the App, the law, or our providers change. The
updated policy will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
