# Privacy Policy for Aelnir PDF Tools

**Effective date: 17 November 2026**

Aelnir ("we", "us") provides the Aelnir PDF Tools mobile application (the
"App"). This policy explains what the App accesses, uses, stores, and shares.

## Summary

The App needs no account and has no server. PDFs and images are opened, edited, and saved on your device, and we never receive your documents or your history. The App asks for no all-files access and no camera permission. The free App shows ads through Google AdMob, whose SDK collects limited device and usage data to provide, measure, and protect advertising.

## Information processed on your device

Depending on what you use, the App processes:

- PDFs, images, and other files you pick in the App or share to it
- The text, pages, and signatures you work with, including text found by OCR
- The settings you choose for each job
- Passwords you type to open or protect a PDF

This stays on your device. Files are opened only when you choose them. Jobs run in isolated processes that hold no permissions, and temporary copies are removed when a job finishes. Passwords are used to open or protect the file and are not stored or sent anywhere.

## Files the App saves

Results are saved to a place you choose or to your device's documents or
pictures. Your original files are not changed. Once saved, those files belong to
you and are managed by Android and your other apps, not by us.

## Device permissions

The App asks for as little as it can:

- **Files and photos:** none. You pick files with Android's own file and photo pickers, so the App only sees what you choose. The App does not request all-files access.
- **Camera:** none. To scan a page, the App can use Google's document scanner from Google Play services, which handles the camera itself.
- **Notifications and foreground service:** show progress and keep a long job running. You can decline notifications.
- **Internet and advertising ID:** load ads and obtain advertising consent, and download OCR language data (see below). Nothing from your documents is sent with them.

You can deny the optional permissions and the rest of the App keeps working.

## Text recognition (OCR)

Text recognition runs on your device. The Latin-script model is built into the
App. For some other languages, the App uses Google ML Kit through Google Play
services, or downloads Tesseract language data once on request. That download is
the only file the App fetches, and it contains none of your data. After it is
downloaded, recognition works offline. Google states that ML Kit may collect
limited usage and performance information; see
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

## Advertising

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform.
Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads
are shown, and you can change your choice later in the App's advertising privacy
choices. If you decline, Google may still show limited or non-personalized ads.

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares:

- IP address (which may be used to estimate general location)
- Product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views
- Diagnostic information about App and SDK performance
- Device identifiers, including the advertising ID where available

Google uses this for advertising (including personalized advertising where you
consent), analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data is
encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

We do not sell personal information and run no analytics, account, or cloud
service of our own. Your documents are never given to the ad service.

## Sharing you choose

When you share or open a file in another app, it goes to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies.

## Retention and deletion

We keep no documents or personal data on any server. Temporary files are removed when a job finishes. Files you save stay until you delete them. Google keeps advertising data according to its policies and controls.

## Children

The App is a general-audience app and is not designed for children under 13. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Your data is processed and stored on your device, and network traffic from the
ad SDK is encrypted in transit. No method is perfectly secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the App, the law, or our providers change. The
updated policy will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
