# Privacy Policy for Aelnir Passport Photo Maker

**Effective date: 17 November 2026**

Aelnir ("we", "us") provides the Aelnir Passport Photo Maker mobile application (the
"App"). This policy explains what the App accesses, uses, stores, and shares.

## Summary

The App needs no account and has no server. Face detection, background replacement, cropping, and export all run on your device, and we never receive your photos. The free App shows ads through Google AdMob, whose SDK collects limited device and usage data to provide, measure, and protect advertising.

## Information processed on your device

Depending on what you use, the App processes:

- Camera images, when you take a portrait in the App
- Photos you choose to import
- Face and background analysis of those photos, done on the device, and the document requirement you pick
- The photos and print sheets the App creates

This stays on your device. Camera frames are used only to show the preview and take your photo. The App does not upload your photos, and we have no server to upload them to. Face detection results are used to frame the photo and are not stored as a profile.

## Files the App saves

Photos and print sheets are saved to your device's pictures folder or to a place
you choose. Exported photos do not include location data. Your originals are not
changed. Once saved, those files belong to you and are managed by Android and
your other apps, not by us.

## Device permissions

The App asks for as little as it can:

- **Camera:** take a portrait. Requested only when you choose to use the camera. You can import a photo instead.
- **Photos:** none. You pick photos with Android's own photo picker, so the App only sees what you choose.
- **Internet and advertising ID:** load ads and obtain advertising consent. Nothing from your photos is sent with them.

You can deny the camera permission and the rest of the App keeps working.

## On-device analysis

Face detection and background segmentation use Google ML Kit models that run on
your device. Google states that ML Kit may collect limited usage and performance
information; see https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.
The App only helps you match the photo requirement you choose. Final acceptance
is decided by the issuing authority.

## Advertising

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform.
Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads
are shown, and you can change your choice later in the App's advertising privacy
choices. If you decline, Google may still show limited or non-personalized ads.

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares:

- IP address (which may be used to estimate general location)
- Product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views
- Diagnostic information about App and SDK performance
- Device identifiers, including the advertising ID where available

Google uses this for advertising (including personalized advertising where you
consent), analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data is
encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

We do not sell personal information and run no analytics, account, or cloud
service of our own. Your photos are never given to the ad service.

## Sharing you choose

When you share a photo or sheet to another app, the file goes to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies.

## Retention and deletion

We keep no photos or personal data on any server. Files you save stay until you delete them. Google keeps advertising data according to its policies and controls.

## Children

The App is a general-audience app and is not designed for children under 13. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Your data is processed and stored on your device, and network traffic from the
ad SDK is encrypted in transit. No method is perfectly secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the App, the law, or our providers change. The
updated policy will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
