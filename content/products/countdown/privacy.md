# Privacy Policy for Aelnir Countdown

**Effective date: 17 November 2026**

Aelnir ("we", "us") provides the Aelnir Countdown mobile application (the
"App"). This policy explains what the App accesses, uses, stores, and shares.

## Summary

The App needs no account and has no server. Your events, reminders, and settings are made and kept on your device, and we never receive them. This version shows no advertising and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

## Information processed on your device

Depending on what you use, the App processes:

- The event names, dates, notes, and repeat rules you enter
- Your reminders and notification history
- Your settings, such as theme and widget choices

This stays on your device. You can delete or archive an event in the App, and uninstalling the App removes its local data.

If you use Android's device backup, some App data may be included in your Google
backup, which Google handles under its own terms. We cannot see it.

## Device permissions

The App asks for as little as it can:

- **Notifications:** remind you before an event. You can decline.
- **Run at startup and wake lock:** restore your reminders after the phone reboots and wake it to deliver them.
- **Network state:** checked by Android components only. The App does not request internet access.

You can deny the optional permissions and the rest of the App keeps working.

## Advertising and analytics

This version of the App shows no ads, has no in-app purchases, and contains no advertising or analytics SDK. If a later version adds any of them, this policy will be updated first.

## Sharing you choose

When you share a countdown card, the image goes to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies.

## Retention and deletion

We keep no events or personal data on any server. Local data stays until you delete it or uninstall the App.

## Children

The App is a general utility and is not designed for children under 13. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Your data is processed and stored on your device, and network traffic from the
ad SDK and Google Play is encrypted in transit. No method is perfectly secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the App, the law, or our providers change. The
updated policy will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
