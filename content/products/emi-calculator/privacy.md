# Privacy Policy for Aelnir EMI Calculator

**Effective date: 17 November 2026**

Aelnir ("we", "us") provides the Aelnir EMI Calculator mobile application (the
"App"). This policy explains what the App accesses, uses, stores, and shares.

## Summary

The App needs no account and has no server. Calculations and saved scenarios are made and kept on your device, and we never receive them. The free App shows ads through Google AdMob, whose SDK collects limited device and usage data to provide, measure, and protect advertising.

## Information processed on your device

Depending on what you use, the App processes:

- The loan amounts, interest rates, tenures, and prepayments you enter into calculators
- Saved scenarios and recent calculations
- Your settings, such as currency and number format

This stays on your device. Currency is a setting only, and no live rates are fetched. You can delete a scenario or your history in the App, and uninstalling the App removes its local data.

## Device permissions

The App asks for as little as it can:

- **Internet, network state, advertising ID, and Google Play Billing:** load ads, obtain advertising consent, and check the Remove Ads purchase. Your loan numbers are not sent with them.

The App shows no runtime permission prompts.

## Estimates

Results are estimates from the numbers you enter. Your lender's figures, which may use different rounding, fees, or day-count rules, are the ones that count.

## Advertising

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform.
Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads
are shown, and you can change your choice later in the App's advertising privacy
choices. If you decline, Google may still show limited or non-personalized ads.

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares:

- IP address (which may be used to estimate general location)
- Product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views
- Diagnostic information about App and SDK performance
- Device identifiers, including the advertising ID where available

Google uses this for advertising (including personalized advertising where you
consent), analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data is
encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

We do not sell personal information and run no analytics, account, or cloud
service of our own. Your loan numbers and scenarios are never given to the ad service.

## Remove Ads purchase

The App offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase through Google Play Billing.
Google Play handles the payment and the purchase record under Google's terms. We
do not receive your payment details. The App checks with Google Play whether
you own the purchase so it can hide ads.

## Sharing you choose

When you share a result or export a schedule as text or CSV, it goes to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies.

## Retention and deletion

We keep no loan data or personal data on any server. Local data stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. Google keeps advertising data according to its policies and controls.

## Children

The App is a general utility and is not designed for children under 13. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Your data is processed and stored on your device, and network traffic from the
ad SDK and Google Play is encrypted in transit. No method is perfectly secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the App, the law, or our providers change. The
updated policy will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
