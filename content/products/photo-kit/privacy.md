# Privacy Policy for Aelnir Photo Kit

**Effective date: 16 November 2026**

Aelnir ("we", "us") provides the Aelnir Photo Kit mobile application (the
"App"). This policy explains what the App accesses, uses, stores, and shares.

## Summary

The App needs no account and has no server. Every photo is compressed, resized,
converted, cropped, cleaned, or turned into a PDF on your device, and we never
receive your photos or your history. The free App shows ads through Google
AdMob, whose SDK collects limited device and usage data to provide, measure,
and protect advertising.

## Information processed on your device

Depending on what you use, the App processes:

- Photos you pick in the App or share to it from another app, including the
  metadata inside them (such as date, camera details, and location, if the
  photo has any)
- The settings you choose for each tool (quality, size, format, page layout)
- The compressed, resized, converted, cropped, or PDF files the App creates

This all stays on your device. Photos are read only when you choose them, and
the App works on temporary copies that are removed when the job finishes. The
App does not upload your photos, and we have no server to upload them to.

## Files the App saves

Results are saved to your device's photo gallery or documents, in an Aelnir
Photo Kit album or the place you choose. Your original photos are not changed.
Once saved, those files belong to you and are managed by Android and your other
apps, not by us. Where a tool offers to remove location data (for example,
Metadata cleaner and Make shareable), it is removed from the new copy, not from
your original.

## History

The App keeps a local history of recent jobs so you can find your results. Each
entry records the tool used, the number and total size of files, the date, the
status, where the results were saved, and the settings used. It does not store
copies of your photos. History stays on your device. You can delete single
items or clear all history in the App, and uninstalling the App removes it.

## Device permissions

The App asks for as little as it can:

- **Photos and files:** none. You pick photos with Android's own photo picker,
  or share them to the App, so the App only sees what you choose.
- **Storage (Android 9 and older only):** save results to your gallery.
- **Notifications:** show progress and completion when a large batch runs in
  the background. You can decline this.
- **Foreground service (data sync):** keep a long batch running while you use
  another app.
- **Internet and advertising ID:** load ads and obtain advertising consent.
  Nothing from your photos or history is sent with them.

You can deny the optional permissions and the rest of the App keeps working.

## Advertising

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform.
Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalized ads
are shown, and you can change your choice later in the App's advertising privacy
choices. If you decline, Google may still show limited or non-personalized ads.

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares:

- IP address (which may be used to estimate general location)
- Product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views
- Diagnostic information about App and SDK performance
- Device identifiers, including the advertising ID where available

Google uses this for advertising (including personalized advertising where you
consent), analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data is
encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

We do not sell personal information and run no analytics, account, or cloud
service of our own. Your photos and their metadata are never given to the ad
service.

## Sharing you choose

When you share a result to another app, or open it in one, the file goes to
the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies.

## Retention and deletion

We keep no photos, history, or personal data on any server. Local history stays
until you delete it or uninstall the App. Files saved to your gallery stay
until you delete them. Google keeps advertising data according to its policies
and controls.

## Children

The App is a general utility and is not designed for children under 13. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Processing happens on your device, and network traffic from the ad SDK is
encrypted in transit. No method is perfectly secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the App, the law, or our providers change. The
updated policy will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
