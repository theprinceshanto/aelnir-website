# Privacy Policy for Jonodut

**Effective date: 17 November 2026**

Aelnir ("we", "us") provides Jonodut, a web service that lets leaders run
websites in Bangla and English, at jonodut.com and app.jonodut.com (the
"Service"). This policy explains what the Service keeps, why, and for how long.
It is a summary of the privacy page published on jonodut.com, which is draft text
that a lawyer will review before wider launch, so wording can change.

## Summary

The Service keeps the account, verification, and billing details it needs to run
a leader's workspace. ID documents are held in private, encrypted storage and are
deleted 90 days after the final decision. We use no advertising trackers.

## What we collect

For your account:

- Your name, username, and email address
- An optional phone number
- Your workspace name and what you told us you will use Jonodut for

For verification:

- Your legal name
- A photo of you and a photo of your ID

For billing:

- The payments you record

We keep you signed in with a session. The Service has no advertising trackers.

## ID documents

ID documents are kept in private, encrypted storage and never appear on a
website, in an email, or in a download. Reviewers open them only to decide, and
every view is recorded. Files are deleted 90 days after the final decision, or
after an appeal closes. We keep a record that the check happened, not the
document.

## People who use a leader's site

When someone signs up to volunteer, joins an event, or sends a message through a
leader's website, it goes to the leader whose site it is, and the leader is
responsible for it. Downloading that list needs its own permission on the
leader's team. Jonodut staff see it only when helping the leader.

## Service providers

The Service runs on hosting, database, storage, content-delivery, and email
providers that process data on our behalf to operate it. Payments are recorded
manually, and the Service does not collect card or mobile-wallet payments itself.

## Email

We email you only what you need: confirmations, decisions, billing and security
notices, and a weekly summary if you turn it on.

## Correcting or removing your details

Ask support to correct or remove your details. Some records, such as the history
of decisions, stay as long as the account exists.

## Children

The Service is for leaders and their teams and is not designed for children
under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Passwords, sessions, and two-step verification protect accounts, and traffic is
encrypted in transit. No method is perfectly secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the Service, the law, or our providers change. The
updated policy will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
